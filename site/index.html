Sign in and play

Steam is ready to sign in. Battle.net, EA, Ubisoft, and Rockstar are ready to install.

  • Steam Counter-Strike
  • Battle.net Blizzard games
  • EA EA games
  • Ubisoft Ubisoft games
  • Rockstar Rockstar games

Why download it

  • Steam opens. You sign in and play.
  • Battle.net, the EA App, Ubisoft Connect, and the Rockstar Launcher are ready too. Install, sign in and play, the same as on Windows.
  • The setup they need is already done.
  • You do not have to learn Linux. It is a desktop, and the games are the point.
  • Games can sit on their own drive and stay there when WASD is reinstalled.

Not supported

Some well-known games will not run here, because of anti-cheat. More information here.
